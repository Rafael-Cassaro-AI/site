The Artist

The artist stays in shadow. The work holds the light.

Rafael Cassaro was born in Santo André, São Paulo, into a family where art was already part of everyday life. His mother was an artist, and the people who raised him drew, painted and worked with their hands. He lost her early; the language she worked in stayed.

Art reached him before he could name it. The women who recur throughout his work were never a subject he set out to choose, he simply understands now where they come from. What was handed down to him, he keeps handing back, one figure at a time.

Over more than twenty years his hand has crossed Europe, South America and North America, rooted in black and grey realism, moving today between classical painting, the Renaissance, the Baroque, and abstraction. He works on canvas as readily as on skin.

I paint the presence I was too young to remember; the work holds her light, and I stay in the shadow.

Rafael Cassaro · Boca Raton, Florida

The Archive

A record, not a feed.

This is not a collection of tattoos. It is a record of trust, time and artistic collaboration. Some projects naturally become part of my journey, not because they were planned as something special, but because the relationship, the process and the final work carried a presence that deserved to be kept. If a piece enters the archive, it earned its place.

Commissions.

Before you inquire

Questions.

How does a session work?
Every project starts with a free consultation. From there the work is done in sessions: a half day covers up to 3 hours, a full day up to 6 hours. Larger pieces are built across several sessions, roughly two weeks apart.
What does it cost?
Pricing is per session, not per hour. Half day is $1,300, full day is $2,000. The number of sessions a piece needs is decided together at the consultation, never quoted over text.
Is there a deposit?
Yes — a $500 deposit reserves your date. On a multi-session project it is held once and applied to your final session. With 48 hours notice it carries to a new date. An Archive Commission is the one exception: it is reserved with a 25% deposit instead.
How can I pay?
Cash, Zelle or card. If you'd rather spread it out, financing is available through Affirm.
How long between sessions?
About two weeks, so the skin heals fully before the next session. Aftercare and guidance are provided at the end of each session.
Do you do cover-ups or a piece you didn't design?
Send a photo of what you have and I'll tell you personally what's possible. Direction is worked out together at the consultation — that's what it's for.
