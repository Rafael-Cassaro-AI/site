Before you submit

What to expect.

The consultation
We sit down, talk the idea through and I sketch the direction. No charge. The scope of a piece is decided here, never over text.
Deposit
A $500 deposit reserves your date. On a multi-session project it is held once and applied to your final session. With 48 hours notice it carries to a new date.
Between sessions
About two weeks, so the skin heals fully before the next one. Aftercare and guidance are given at the end of every session.
Payment
Cash, Zelle or card. If you would rather spread it out, financing is available through Affirm.
Creative direction
The work is authored. You bring the idea, the meaning and the references; the composition is drawn by me, for you, and made once.
References
Send anything that carries the feeling you are after, images, music, a photograph that matters to you. They guide the drawing, they are not copied.
Cover-ups and existing work
Send a photo of what you have and I will tell you personally what is possible. Direction is worked out together at the consultation, that is what it is for.
Colour
The foundation is black and grey. Touches of colour are possible as an accent when the piece asks for it.
Touch-ups
Complimentary, once the piece has fully healed.
Where
Boca Raton, Florida. The full address and parking details come with your confirmation.
